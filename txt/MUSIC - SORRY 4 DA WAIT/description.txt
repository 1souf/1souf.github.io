As a longtime Carti fan, "MUSIC" was, in my opinion, his weakest release. Not because I didn’t like the songs, no. I’ve enjoyed every sound Carti has gone for so far. My issues with the album are purely down to the weird decisions and poor mixes that are present throughout the project.

I’m going to fix and change everything I can think of that annoys me when listening to this album. I’m not trying to make any changes to the tracklist; the goal is simply to improve the existing songs and overall listening experience.

Some of the changes may be extremely minor, which goes to show just how little care was put into this album before its release.
