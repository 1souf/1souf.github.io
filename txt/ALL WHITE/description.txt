After releasing their studio album "Eternal Atake 2", Lil Uzi Vert quickly started what fans saw as a new era known as "ALL WHITE". It was defined by Uzi’s all-white outfits, fast and off-grid flows, and his habit of remixing songs across different genres. The whole era seemed to point toward a new sound and creative direction for Uzi, which led many fans to see it as a possible comeback after "Eternal Atake 2".

Unfortunately, the project was eventually shelved indefinitely, without there ever being a clear reason for it. Because of that, many of the snippets teased during the era still haven’t been officially released, leaving fans disappointed and wondering what could have become of the project.
