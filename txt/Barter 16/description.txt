A realistic approach of what could've been Lil Uzi Vert's scrapped album "Barter 16". Includes every single song that has ever appeared on any of the confirmed tracklists.

The edits on this are minimal, because I do not want to rework the original idea of the album too much, a lot of songs are unchanged or just include tiny fixes.

This comp is the perfect opportunity for anyone who wants to listen to this album in a realistic order without having to scrape it together from the leaks themselves.
